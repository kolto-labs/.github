# Code of Conduct

Kolto Labs has adopted the **[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)** as its code of conduct. It applies in every Kolto Labs space: repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions, our Discord once it opens, and anywhere someone represents the project.

What follows is a summary. The linked text is the one that counts.

## Our pledge

We want participation in Kolto Labs to be harassment-free for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behaviour

- Be kind and considerate. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Respect differing opinions, viewpoints and experience levels.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes, apologise to those affected, and learn from them.
- Focus on what's best for the community, not just for you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information, such as an address or real name, without their explicit permission.
- Anything else that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Project-specific notes

- **No gatekeeping.** Newcomer questions get answers, not "search the forum". If the answer isn't written down anywhere, that's our bug.
- **No piracy.** Don't share, request or link to pirated copies of any game, in any space. It's a conduct issue as well as a policy one.
- **Credit others.** Passing off someone else's work, research or tools as your own is a conduct issue.

## Reporting

There's no conduct mailbox yet. Two routes work today:

- On any comment, issue or pull request, use GitHub's **Report content** menu item. That report goes to GitHub's own trust and safety staff.
- To reach the maintainers, open an issue titled `Conduct: need a private channel` with no names and no details. A maintainer will reply there with a private way to talk.

Reports to the maintainers are kept confidential, and nobody gets punished for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers follow the Covenant's enforcement ladder:

1. **Correction:** a private, written warning explaining what happened.
2. **Warning:** a warning with consequences for continued behaviour, including a period of no interaction.
3. **Temporary ban:** from all community interaction for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban:** from all community interaction.
